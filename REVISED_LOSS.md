| New Category | Old Category | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Loss of bodily integrity | Loss of a limb or its use | Covers the loss, amputation, mutilation or impairment of a limb, organ, eye or other part of the body, including impairment of bodily function as a whole. |
| Loss of the senses | Loss of ability to walk or see | Covers loss or reduction of sight, hearing, smell, taste or touch. |
| Loss from scarring or disfigurement | — | Covers the effect of visible scarring, burns, deformity or disfigurement on the plaintiff’s enjoyment of life, including embarrassment, self-consciousness, reduced confidence, social withdrawal or avoidance of activities previously enjoyed. |
| Loss of mobility | Loss of ability to walk or see<br>- Loss of a limb or its use | Covers the inability or reduced ability to enjoy independent movement, including walking, taking strolls, going on outings, climbing stairs, cycling, travelling freely or moving about without restriction. It may range from minor limitation to dependence on a wheelchair or severe paralysis. |
| Loss of independence | Loss of ability to walk or see<br>- Loss of a limb or its use | Covers the reduced enjoyment, autonomy and dignity caused by needing assistance with personal care, dressing, washing, feeding oneself, moving safely or living independently. |
| Loss of ability to do domestic tasks | Loss of a limb or its use | Covers the inability or reduced ability to enjoy ordinary home life and household routines, including cooking, cleaning, shopping, childcare, gardening, home maintenance or other domestic activities previously undertaken with satisfaction or independence. |
| Loss of mental ability | Loss of mental ability | Covers the reduced enjoyment of life caused by impaired memory, concentration, reasoning, understanding, intellectual ability or mental capacity, including difficulty engaging in activities, conversations, decisions or interests as before. |
| Loss of communication ability | — | Covers the reduced ability to enjoy social interaction and self-expression through speaking, writing, reading, understanding language or communicating effectively with others. |
| Personality change | Personality change | Covers the effect of changes in mood, behaviour, temperament or character on the plaintiff’s enjoyment of life and relationships, including irritability, aggression, anxiety, emotional instability, withdrawal or reduced ability to relate to others. |
| Loss of confidence in going out in the public | Loss of confidence in going out in the public | Covers reduced enjoyment of ordinary public life due to fear, anxiety, embarrassment or loss of confidence when leaving home or being in public places, including avoidance of crowds, social activities or everyday outings. |
| Loss of ability to enjoy food and drink | — | Covers the inability or reduced ability to taste or digest certain foods, affecting enjoyment of meals. |
| Loss of ability to enjoy quiet or solitude | — | Covers increased sensitivity to noise or inability to find peace due to psychological distress. |
| Loss of family life | Loss of the pleasures of family or social life | Covers the inability or reduced ability to enjoy family life, including family activities, outings, routines, shared responsibilities, conversation, affection and ordinary interaction within the family. |
| Loss of ability to participate in parenthood or grandparenthood | - Loss of pleasure in being able to look after one's grandchildren<br>- Loss of pleasure in being able to play with one's children<br>- Deprivation of the general pleasures of parenthood | Covers the inability or reduced ability to enjoy and participate in ordinary parental or grandparental life, including playing with, lifting, caring for, bonding with, guiding or supporting children or grandchildren, attending their activities and sharing milestones. |
| Loss of marriage prospects | Loss of marriage prospects | Covers the loss or reduction of prospects of marrying or forming a long-term relationship because of injury, disability, disfigurement or psychological consequences. |
| Breakdown of the family | Breakdown of the family | Covers the breakdown of a marriage, civil partnership, cohabiting relationship or family unit caused or materially contributed to by the injury. |
| Loss of sexual function and sexual life | Loss of sexual function | Covers the inability or reduced ability to enjoy or participate in sexual relations and activities, including pain, impotence, loss of libido or psychological inhibition arising from the injury. |
| Loss of ability to give birth | Loss of ability to give birth | Covers the loss of enjoyment, fulfilment or life experience associated with bearing children, where infertility, loss of reproductive capacity or other injury-related consequences prevent the plaintiff from having children. |
| Loss of ability to give natural childbirth | Loss of ability to give natural childbirth | Covers the loss of the opportunity or experience of vaginal childbirth, where the plaintiff is unable to give birth naturally and requires medical intervention such as a caesarean section, or otherwise cannot experience childbirth as anticipated. |
| Loss of social life | Loss of the pleasures of family or social life<br>- Loss of the ability to engage in social activities, sports, and hobbies | Covers the reduced ability to enjoy socialising and ordinary social participation, including visiting friends, attending clubs, places of worship, restaurants, concerts, sporting events, community events or maintaining an active social life. |
| Loss of ability to engage in sports and hobbies | Loss of the ability to engage in social activities, sports and hobbies | Covers the inability or reduced ability to pursue hobbies, sport, dancing, crafts, music, gardening, driving, reading, playing an instrument, attending clubs or other recreational activities that previously gave pleasure. |
| Loss of holidays or special occasions | Loss of the ability to engage in social activities, sports and hobbies | Covers the loss, interruption or reduced enjoyment of a holiday, trip, celebration, social event or other occasion the plaintiff was looking forward to. |
| Loss of congenial employment | - Loss of congenial employment<br>- Loss of pride and pleasure in one's work<br>- Loss of the ability to pursue a rewarding and enjoyable occupation | Covers the inability or reduced ability to continue in or pursue a job, profession, vocation or chosen occupation that the plaintiff found enjoyable, meaningful or rewarding, including loss of job satisfaction, career fulfilment, professional identity, purpose or opportunities for advancement. |
| Loss of ability to pursue education | — | Covers the inability or reduced ability to continue studies or acquire new skills. |
| Loss of ability to volunteer or engage in community service | Loss of the ability to engage in social activities, sports and hobbies | Covers the inability or reduced ability to contribute to society or participate in charitable activities. |
| Loss of expectation of life | Loss of expectation of life | Covers the shortening of the plaintiff’s life expectancy as a result of the injury. |
| Other | — | Covers any residual loss or curtailment of the plaintiff’s ability to enjoy life as he did before the injury, including reduced participation in everyday pleasures, routines, experiences, relationships, recreation, independence, confidence, and personal fulfilment. |
